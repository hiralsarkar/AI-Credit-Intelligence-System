Uplift / Next-Best-Action

Exploratory · observational · not causal

Loan modification is used here as an observed, not randomly assigned, treatment. The estimated effect is therefore confounded by selection into treatment, and the uplift-decile validation is not monotonic. Read this as a targeting hypothesis, not a proven intervention effect. Do not quote the ATE as a treatment effect.

Which delinquent accounts are persuadable - where a collections action changes the outcome, rather than accounts that would cure (or default) either way? A two-model uplift learner ranks them so limited collections capacity goes where it moves the needle.

Method: two-model T-learner

Fit one model on treated accounts and one on control accounts, each predicting P(avoid default). The predicted uplift for an account is the difference between the two: how much modification is expected to raise its odds of curing. Accounts are then ranked by predicted uplift so collections targets the persuadables first.

Validation: honest, and only partial

If the model ranked persuadables well, the observed treated-minus-control gap would rise monotonically across deciles of predicted uplift. It does not: the signal concentrates in the top deciles and is flat or negative below them.

What this supports: targeting the top ~20% of accounts by predicted uplift is defensible - that band shows a real treated-vs-control gap. Ranking across the full population is not yet reliable.

Why observational uplift is dangerous

Banks tend to modify accounts that were already more likely to cure (engaged borrowers, softer hardship). Treated accounts then look better than controls even if modification did nothing, so the naive ATE overstates the true effect. Selection into treatment, not the treatment itself, drives part of the +4.2pp.

What a production build needs

A randomised champion/challenger holdout (withhold treatment from a random slice) or, failing that, propensity-score adjustment / doubly-robust estimation to strip out the selection effect. Only then does the uplift ranking earn the right to drive spend.
